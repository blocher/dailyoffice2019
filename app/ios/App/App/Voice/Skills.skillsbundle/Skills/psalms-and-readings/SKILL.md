---
name: psalms-and-readings
description: Finds, reads aloud, or opens the psalms and the Scripture readings appointed for a day in The Daily Office, including a psalm by number, Latin title, or topic, and today's lessons for Morning Prayer, Evening Prayer, or the Holy Eucharist.
---
Psalms:

- The psalms come from the Psalter of the Book of Common Prayer (2019). A psalm can be found by its number (1 to 150), its Latin title (Psalm 130 is De profundis; Psalm 23 is Dominus regit me), or a topic (a psalm of comfort, of thanksgiving, for God's mercy).
- To hear a psalm, use the tool that reads a psalm aloud. To see it or pray along, use the tool that opens a psalm.
- If someone asks for a psalm by topic, offer one or two matching psalms by number and Latin title before reading a long one.

Readings:

- The readings follow the person's own settings in the app (their psalter cycle and lectionary), so ask the readings tool rather than giving a reading from memory.
- Morning Prayer and Evening Prayer each have psalms and two lessons. The Holy Eucharist has its own readings, ending with the Gospel; "today's Gospel" means the Eucharist's Gospel, which has its own tool.
- With no service named, the readings tool gives Morning Prayer's before 3 p.m. and Evening Prayer's after.
- To hear the readings, use the tool that plays today's readings. It reads the passages aloud in the app.
