---
name: pray-the-office
description: Helps someone pray or listen to the Daily Office or Family Prayer from the Book of Common Prayer (2019) in The Daily Office, choosing the right office for the time of day, the household, and whether they want to listen or read along.
---
The Daily Office has eight offices. The Daily Office proper is Morning Prayer, Midday Prayer, Evening Prayer, and Compline. Family Prayer is a shorter set for households and children: Family Prayer in the Morning, at Midday, in the Early Evening, and at the Close of Day.

Choosing the office:

- If the person names an office, use it. Matins means Morning Prayer; Evensong and Vespers mean Evening Prayer; night prayer or bedtime prayers mean Compline.
- If they ask to pray "the office", "my prayers", or "the next office" without naming one, leave the office empty; the app chooses by the time of day (Morning Prayer from 4 a.m., Midday Prayer from 11, Evening Prayer from 3 p.m., Compline from 8 p.m.).
- If they mention their family, children, kids, the household, a meal, or praying together at home, use Family Prayer. "Family prayer" with no time means Family Prayer for this time of day; bedtime with children means Family Prayer at the Close of Day.
- If they say they missed an office ("I missed Morning Prayer"), give them the office they named, not the current one.

Listening or reading:

- Use the play tool when they want to listen, hear, or have it read aloud, or when they are driving, walking, or have their hands busy. It opens the office and starts the audio.
- Use the open tool when they want to read, see, or pray along with the text, or say they are leading others.
- Today's Scripture readings alone (without the rest of the office) have their own play and open tools.

Audio is read in English for the ESV and KJV translations, from three days ago to nine days ahead. If someone uses another translation or language, the office opens with its text and the app explains that audio isn't available; say so rather than promising audio.

Don't recite or make up the words of an office yourself. The app has the texts appointed for the day, including the day's psalms, lessons, and collect.
